RECOMMENDATION
Adopt first reading of an Ordinance setting the FY 2027 tax rate for funding Sustainable
and Accountable Funding for Equitable Street Transformation, Revitalization,
Enhancements, and Essential Traffic Safety (SAFE STREETS), otherwise known as
Measure FF, at an annual rate of $0.17842 per square foot of dwelling unit improvements
and $0.26238 per square foot for industrial, commercial, and institutional improvements.

FISCAL IMPACTS OF RECOMMENDATION
The FY 2026 tax of $0.1700 per square foot of dwelling unit improvements and $0.2500
per square foot for non-dwelling unit improvements is being adjusted by the greater of the
Personal Income Growth increase in California of 4.95% against the Consumer Price
Index in the immediate San Francisco Bay Area of 3.7975%.

The annual tax rate of $0.17842 per square foot of dwelling unit improvements and
$0.26238 per square foot for industrial, commercial, and institutional improvements (cited
as “Non-Residential” in table below) is projected to yield $16,651,651 in revenues. The
overall tax rate for the current year is lower than the prior year; however, the prior year’s
rate was based on an 18-month revenue period from January 1, 2025, when the tax first
became effective, through June 30, 2026, whereas the current year’s rate is based on a
standard 12-month period from July 1 through June 30. It is estimated that the tax will
cost taxpayers the following amounts for FY 2026 and FY 2027:

CURRENT SITUATION AND ITS EFFECTS
Through Measure FF, as approved by voters in November 2024, the Council is authorized
to impose and allocate a special tax as follows:

      Residential Rate: For all dwelling units, the tax may be imposed at the rate of up
       to $0.17842 per square foot of improvements.
      Non-Residential Rate: For all other property, the tax may be imposed at the rate
       of up to $0.26238 per square foot of improvements.

This rate was initially adopted through the adoption of Ordinance 7,952-N.S., on
December 10, 2024. The Council may impose the tax authorized by this Chapter up to
the rate, and subject to inflation adjustments, set forth in Section 7.11.020 of the Berkeley
Municipal Code.

BACKGROUND
Measure FF, the Sustainable and Accountable Funding for Equitable Street
Transformation, Revitalization, Enhancements, and Essential Traffic Safety (SAFE
STREETS) Initiative, was passed by voters on November 5, 2024, and adopted as
Ordinance 7,952-N.S. on December 10, 2024.

The SAFE STREETS Initiative authorized an annual parcel tax for a 14-year period. This
measure is projected to generate approximately $15 million annually, dedicated to funding
street and sidewalk repairs, safety improvements, and environmental infrastructure
projects.

ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY AND CLIMATE IMPACTS
There are no identifiable environmental opportunities or impacts associated with the
administration of this tax.

RATIONALE FOR RECOMMENDATION
Ordinance 7,952-N.S., as adopted on December 10, 2024, authorizes the tax rate as
described therein, to remain in place from the effective date, unless extended by voters.

                              ORDINANCE NO. #,###-N.S.

SETTING THE FISCAL YEAR 2027 SPECIAL TAX RATE TO FUND THE SUSTAINABLE
AND ACCOUNTABLE FUNDING FOR EQUITABLE STREET TRANSFORMATION,
REVITALIZATION, ENHANCEMENTS, AND ESSENTIAL TRAFFIC SAFETY (SAFE
STREETS) INITIATIVE, MEASURE FF

BE IT ORDAINED by the Council of the City of Berkeley as follows:

Section 1. The rate of tax for FY 2027 to provide for the costs incurred for the funding
street and sidewalk repairs, safety improvements, and environmental infrastructure
projects (Measure FF) is hereby fixed and apportioned upon the square feet of
improvements as defined in the Ordinance as follows:

$0.17842 per square foot of dwelling unit improvements and $0.26238 per square foot for
industrial, commercial, and institutional building improvements (Non-Residential).

Section 2. The cost to taxpayers during FY 2027 will be $214.10 for a 1,200 square foot
dwelling and $535.26 for a 3,000 square foot dwelling. For other non-residential
properties, during FY 2027, the cost will be $314.86 for a 1,200 square foot dwelling and
$787.14 for a 3,000 square foot dwelling.

Section 3. This tax rate will result in estimated total collection of $16,651,651.

Section 4. Copies of this Ordinance shall be posted for two days prior to adoption in the
display case located near the walkway in front of the Maudelle Shirek Building, 2134
Martin Luther King Jr. Way. Within 15 days of adoption, copies of this Ordinance shall be
filed at each branch of the Berkeley Public Library and the title shall be published in a
newspaper of general circulation.
