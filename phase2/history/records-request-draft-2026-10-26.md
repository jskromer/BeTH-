# Berkeley street funding and expenditures — draft records request

Prepared October 26, 2026. Approved as drafted by Steve Kromer on October 27, 2026. **Not submitted**; submission awaits a separate instruction.

To: City of Berkeley, City Clerk / Public Records

Please provide the following existing records concerning street funding, expenditures and the Council actions identified below. Electronic records in their existing machine-readable format (such as CSV or Excel) are preferred, with associated code dictionaries. Please retain fiscal year, fund and project identifiers. This request seeks existing records and calculations; it does not ask the City to create a new analysis.

1. **Street project appropriations and actual expenditures, FY2014–FY2026.** Existing project-level accounting reports or exports for street rehabilitation, reconstruction, overlay/sealing and patching, including Measure M and T1 street projects. Please include original and amended appropriations, actual expenditures, outstanding encumbrances and remaining balances as separately recorded; funding fund, project and account codes; and transaction dates or fiscal years. Please include the existing crosswalk identifying which projects belong to the street rehabilitation program. Where available, retain construction, design, engineering, administration and maintenance categories separately. Please identify any provisional FY2026 figures in the records provided.

2. **Annual Street Reports and classification support, FY2010–FY2025.** The City's filings to the State Controller and existing workpapers or fund/account-to-category crosswalks supporting them. In particular, please provide records explaining the following entries filed as Street Reconstruction: FY2024 fund 305, “Private Admin & Eng – PWTRCS2002,” $3,818,649; FY2024 fund 149, $275,960; FY2025 fund 501, “Private Admin & Eng – PWENST2301,” $7,371,484; and FY2025 fund 501, “Force Maintenance – PWENEN2001,” $629,647. Please also provide existing records explaining the FY2014 classification of patching and reconstruction and FY2025 overlay/sealing. These descriptions identify entries for retrieval; they do not assume the entries were classified incorrectly.

3. **Workers' Compensation funding actions, FY2023–FY2026.** Journal entries, interfund receivable/payable schedules, loan agreements and repayment records implementing or referring to (a) the June 13, 2023 Council action authorizing $4,050,000 toward the Measure T1 funding gap and (b) Resolution 70,997-N.S., July 25, 2023, authorizing a $5,500,000 internal loan for Marina D and E dock replacement. Please include source and destination funds, related project codes, amendments or superseding actions, and waterfront transient occupancy tax repayment entries. Please include existing support identifying which authorization, if either, is associated with the FY2024 $5,500,000 transfer out of Workers' Compensation to the General Fund, and records supporting the different source-fund label in the FY2024 ACFR transfer note. We are seeking the recorded disposition, without assuming either authorization was executed as written.

4. **Measure FF maintenance-of-effort calculation.** Existing calculations, schedules and adopted-budget crosswalks for the $15.3 million requirement in BMC 7.11.030, for FY2025–FY2028 to the extent held. Please include the fiscal-year base, inflation index and annual factors, the funds and activities counted, and any application of the external-source adjustment or suspension provisions. Please include existing nonprivileged guidance on whether Zero Waste fund 601 and Clean Storm fund 616 count toward the requirement, and the records showing whether and how they were counted. This requests existing records, not a new legal opinion.

5. **Resolution 70,456-N.S. baseline and escalation workpapers.** Existing worksheets and supporting records for the $7.3 million baseline, the additional $8 million policy contribution, and the “General Fund: $9.9M/yr” baseline shown on slide 37 of the March 25, 2025 Safe Streets workshop. Please include the source breakdown, base year and annual index factors; records reconciling the June 2022 budget increases of $5.1 million and $9 million to subsequent plans; and records explaining the FY2027 policy amounts of $9,481,286 in the November 2023 plan and $8,487,200 in the July 2026 plan, including any budget amendments or rebasing worksheets.

6. **Fund and project code dictionaries.** Existing dictionaries or crosswalks for funds 127, 133, 134, 147, 149, 305, 501, 506, 511, 601, 616 and 630, and the PW/project identifiers used in the Annual Street Reports and street-project accounting records. Please retain applicable dates, renamed or closed codes, project names, and any existing link to CIP project identifiers.

Please provide records on a rolling basis if some items can be fulfilled sooner. If responsive records are already public, direct links to the specific files or datasets would be helpful. Please notify me of any anticipated copying or processing charge before incurring it.

## Review context

The six numbered items correspond to the unresolved evidence gaps in [the research findings](findings-2026-10-26-street-expenditures.md). The State Controller series is City-reported street-purpose spending, with broader scope than paving alone. This request is intended to reconcile those categories to the City's project accounting, distinguish authorizations from transactions, and obtain the City's own maintenance-of-effort calculations.
