C.	 Advances to/from
During 2017, the General Fund borrowed $6,683,398 from the Workers Compensation Fund to fund the
purchase of a building located at 1001, 1007, and 1011 University Avenue and 1925 Ninth Street. Repayment
of the loan will be funded from a combination of the Business License Tax of five or more units and excess
Property Transfer Tax. Interest on the loan is the State of California Local Agency Investment Fund rate of
0.68 percent. No principal repayment of the loan was made during FY 2023, leaving a principal balance
outstanding of $813,779 as of June 30, 2023.
