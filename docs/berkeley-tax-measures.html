Measure MNovember 2022
General tax (vacancy)
An empty-homes tax on long-vacant residential units.
Passed 64.9% · Levied; contested
Legally uncertain — San Francisco’s comparable empty-homes tax was struck down by a trial court in October 2024 and the City’s appeal is pending. Berkeley sent its first bills in September 2025 (for 2024 vacancies); as of April 2026 about $1.5M was unpaid by owners waiting on the appeal. Sunsets December 31, 2034. Not to be confused with the 2012 Measure M (streets/watershed bond).

Recent measures that did not pass (and are not collected): Measure GG 2024 (gas/emissions tax), Measure HH 2020 (utility-tax increase), and Measure EE 2024 (the competing street tax). Council-set taxes with no ballot measure include the Utility Users Tax (7.5%), the hotel tax (12%), and the base Business License Tax. School-district and regional taxes (BUSD, EBRPD Measure WW, AC Transit, BART, county Measures B/BB) are not City of Berkeley measures.
