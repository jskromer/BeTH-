Flagged: (1) Item 1 (2700 Shattuck): staff present two mutually exclusive options. Option 1 finds the project was "deemed approved" on July 2, 2026 under the Permit Streamlining Act with all three density-bonus concessions — including the waiver of the inclusionary in-lieu fee — which staff say would mean a $2,110,274 loss to the Housing Trust Fund. Option 2 rejects the deemed approval and affirms ZAB's denial of the fee waiver, preserving the $2,110,274. Staff do not recommend one option over the other. (2) The fee is payable before the first certificate of occupancy, so even under Option 2 the revenue is years out and contingent on construction. (3) A separate, already-granted concession exempts the project from the public-art in-lieu fee (BMC 23.316); no dollar value is stated anywhere in the packet — Unknown. (4) The ZAB conditions of approval (Attachment 1) put the fee at $2,029,109.35 "subject to proration," versus the staff report's $2,110,274 "at the current in-lieu fee rate" — an $81,165 gap, presumably a fee-rate update; the final amount is set at building-permit issuance. (5) The conditions also require a $22,000 compliance-monitoring deposit from the applicant (cost recovery, not net revenue). (6) Correction to the Nov 17 scan's note that the Nov 12 agenda "lists no dollar figures": true of the agenda face ("See report"), but the staff report quantifies the stake. Basis for the $2,110,274: BMC 23.328 requires 50 on-site affordable units for this project; the applicant proposes 38 Very Low Income units and would owe a pro-rated in-lieu fee for the 12-unit shortfall at the current fee rate (staff report, "Fiscal Impacts of Recommendation" and Issue 3 response). The applicant requested a waiver of that fee as its third density-bonus concession; ZAB denied it on July 9, 2026 and the applicant appealed on July 24. A second appeal (East Bay Residents for Responsible Development, July 27) concerns AB 130 labor standards, not fees.
