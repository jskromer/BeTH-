Governmental funds: total revenues, total expenditures (including capital outlay and debt service), fund balance. Enterprise and internal service funds: operating revenues, operating expenses (including depreciation), total net position. Transfers in are shown separately; they are not revenue. Figures read by tools/acfr_funds.py from the FY2025 ACFR statement pages named on each fund page; row and column sums were checked against the statements' own totals. "Ledger" marks funds whose dated flow events have been read from Council documents.

Fund # is the City's budget fund number from Exhibit A of the FY2027 and FY2026 Annual Appropriations Ordinances. The audit names funds but does not number them, so each match was made by name (hover a number for notes; "?" marks a low-confidence match). Some audit columns combine several budget funds (General Fund, Library, Grants, Equipment). "—" means the fund has no appropriation in either year's budget. Council agenda items cite these numbers; see Council actions by fund to follow them meeting by meeting. Crosswalk: data/fund_numbers.json.
