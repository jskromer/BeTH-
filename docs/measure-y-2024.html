What it taxes

Raises the Parks, Trees & Landscaping tax to $0.2652 per sq ft of improvements, inflation-adjusted. About $22M per year in total.

What it funds

Maintenance and staffing of 58 parks, right-of-way landscaping, and all City street and park trees.

An increase on the existing Parks Tax; the prior increase was Measure F in 2014.
