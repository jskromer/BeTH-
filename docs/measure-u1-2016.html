What it taxes

Raises the gross-receipts business-license tax on owners of 5 or more residential rental units to 2.880% (sitting tenants exempt; new units exempt 12 years). Budgeted at $6.6M for FY2027.

What it funds

General Fund — the Council directs the revenue toward affordable housing and anti-homelessness programs.
