A monthly civic recap

Council, Decoded

Once a month, in under five minutes: what Berkeley’s City Council actually did with your money. The dollars that moved, three items worth knowing, and what’s on the horizon — in plain language, drawn straight from the council agenda.

What each issue covers

The same five-part spine, every month

  1. The one-line hook
    A single concrete figure or decision from this month’s agenda — the thing a resident would find surprising or useful.
  2. The numbers, decoded
    The month’s money in plain language: new spending, incoming revenue, pass-through (collected but not spent), and the net impact on city spending.
  3. Three items worth knowing
    Three specific agenda items — who’s getting paid, for what, and why it matters — chosen for relevance, not dollar size.
  4. On the horizon
    The policy referrals with no dollars attached yet — where next year’s spending and policy are heading.
  5. What it means
    A short read on the through-line: what the month tells you about how the city sets priorities and spends.
How it’s built. Roughly 80% of each issue comes straight from the council agenda, parsed into structured spending, revenue, and referral data. The judgment — what mattered, what to watch — is added by hand. Every figure traces back to the dated agenda packet.
