What it taxes

$0.17 per sq ft of improvements on dwellings and $0.25 per sq ft on other property, inflation-adjustable. About $15M per year for 14 years (~$267M).

What it funds

Street, sidewalk and path repair (60%), traffic safety (30%), and green infrastructure (10%). Funds begin in FY2026.

A citizen initiative that passed by simple majority. The competing council measure (Measure EE) failed.
