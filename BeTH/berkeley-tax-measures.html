Berkeley's Tax Measures

The voter-approved taxes and bonds currently funding the City — what each does, and a link to the actual election item that passed. Special taxes and bonds needed a two-thirds vote; general taxes a simple majority.

Special parcel taxes

Per-square-foot taxes on building improvements, dedicated to a purpose. Required a two-thirds vote.

Measure GGNov 2008
Fire / disaster & EMS
$0.04083/sq ft (dwellings), $0.06179/sq ft (other); escalates yearly.
Keeps fire stations staffed, paramedic first response, CERT disaster prep, emergency radio.
Passed 71.7% · operative
Election result →
Measure FFNov 2020
Emergency response
$0.1047/sq ft of improvements; escalates. ~$8.5M/yr.
Firefighting, EMS, 9-1-1, wildfire prevention & preparedness.
Passed 74.2% · operative
Election result → City page
Measure FFNov 2024
Streets & sidewalks
$0.17/sq ft (dwellings), $0.25/sq ft (other). ~$15M/yr for 14 yrs (~$267M).
Street/sidewalk/path repair (60%), traffic safety (30%), green infrastructure (10%). Funds begin FY2026.
Passed 60.9% (citizen initiative, majority) · operative
Election result → City page
Measure XNov 2024
Library
Adds 6¢/sq ft (residential), up to 9¢ (non-res) to the Library Services Tax. ~$5.6M/yr.
Berkeley Public Library operations (~99.8% of its operating cost). The library tax dates to the 1980 Library Relief Act.
Passed 78.6% · operative
Election result →
Measure YNov 2024
Parks & trees
Parks tax raised to $0.2652/sq ft of improvements. ~$22M/yr total.
Maintenance & staffing of 58 parks, street landscaping, all City trees. (Predecessor: Measure F, 2014.)
Passed 75.2% · operative
Election result → Measure F (2014)

General obligation bonds

Borrowing for capital projects, repaid by a property tax on assessed value. Required a two-thirds vote.

Measure MNov 2012
Streets & watershed bond
Up to $30M; debt-service tax over ~30 yrs.
Street repair + green infrastructure (rain gardens, bioswales) to cut flooding and protect the Bay.
Passed 73.3% · operative
Election result → City page
Measure T1Nov 2016
Infrastructure bond
Up to $100M; ~$21 per $100k assessed value (avg).
Repair aging infrastructure & facilities — sidewalks, storm drains, parks, streets, senior/rec centers, City buildings.
Passed 86.6% · operative
Election result → City page
Measure ONov 2018
Affordable housing bond
Up to $135M; ~$23 per $100k assessed value (avg).
Acquire, build, rehab & preserve affordable housing for low- to middle-income households.
Passed 77.5% · operative
Election result → City page

General taxes

Revenue to the General Fund. Required a simple majority. (Council may direct the spending, but it isn't legally dedicated.)

Measure SNov 2010
Cannabis tax
Authorizes up to 10% gross receipts; Council sets the actual rate (cut to ~5% in 2018).
General Fund.
Passed 83.0% · operative
Election result →
Measure DNov 2014
Soda tax
1¢ per fluid ounce on distributors of sugar-sweetened beverages — first in the U.S.
General Fund (a citizen panel advises on health uses).
Passed ~76% · operative
Election result →
Measure U1Nov 2016
Business license tax
Gross-receipts tax on owners of 5+ rental units raised to 2.880% (sitting tenants exempt). ~$3–4M/yr.
General Fund — Council directs toward affordable housing / anti-homelessness.
Passed 74.9% · operative
Election result →
Measure PNov 2018
Transfer tax
Transfer tax raised to 2.5% on sales over ~$1.5M.
General Fund — directed to homeless services.
Passed ~72% · operative (rate now collected; superseded by Measure W in 2027)
Election result → City page
Measure WNov 2024
Transfer tax
Tiered: 2.5% / 3.0% / 3.5% on higher-value sales. Removes Measure P's 2029 sunset (makes it permanent).
Homeless services. Takes effect Jan 1, 2027.
Passed 60.9% · takes effect 2027
Election result → City page
Measure MNov 2022
Empty-homes (vacancy) tax
$3,000–$12,000/yr on units vacant >182 days. Effective 2024.
General Fund / housing.
Passed 64.9%
Under legal challenge — a comparable San Francisco vacancy tax was struck down on appeal. Currently levied; durability uncertain.
Election result →

Other & context

A spending authorization, taxes the Council sets without a measure, and the regional/school taxes that are not City of Berkeley taxes.

Measure AANov 2024
Spending authorization (not a tax)
Raises no taxes.
Lets the City spend existing special-tax revenue above the state "Gann" appropriations limit (FY2026–FY2029). Must be renewed every 4 years.
Operative

Operative · council-set
Not City taxes—
Regional & school
Berkeley property owners also pay school-district taxes (BSEP/Measure H, school bonds) and regional taxes (EBRPD Measure WW, AC Transit, BART Measure RR, county Measures B/BB). These are not City of Berkeley measures.
Recent measures that did not pass (and are not collected): Measure GG 2024 (gas/emissions tax), Measure HH 2020 (utility-tax increase), and Measure EE 2024 (the competing street-repair tax). The only operative "GG" is the 2008 fire tax above.
